# unibucbeamer 0.2.0

* The YAML setup now lives in the output formats `ub_presentation()` and
  `ub_prezentare()`, so the template header only needs title, author and date.
  Pandoc variables set in the header still take precedence.
* Source Sans 3 is bundled and used as the Beamer font.
* PowerPoint templates (Romanian and English) with the same design, in the
  repository's `PowerPoint/` folder.
* Removed a white hairline at the slide edges.
* `inst/COPYRIGHTS` documents the licences of the bundled font and the
  University of Bucharest logo and artwork.

# unibucbeamer 0.1.0

* First version as an R package, with R Markdown templates `ub-beamer-en`
  and `ub-beamer-ro`.
* MIT licence, excluding the University of Bucharest logo and artwork.
